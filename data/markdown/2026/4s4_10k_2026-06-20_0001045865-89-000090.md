# Annual Report

## Business Overview

4S4 Hybrid Application Consulting is a South African technology consultancy specialising in enterprise application consulting, system integration, and managed services. The company was founded at the outset of the COVID-19 pandemic by its principal founder, who self-funded the business from inception and has continued to do so without external capital. 4S4 is headquartered at Ambassade, Block D, De Wagenweg Office Park, Stellentia Road, Stellenbosch, 7600, South Africa, and may be reached at +27 (10) 035 4062 or info@4s4.io.

The company's name derives from four foundational values — Strength, Service, Spirit, and Soul — which the founder articulates as the governing philosophy of the enterprise. Strength refers to integrity of character; Service to the primacy of client outcomes; Spirit to the manner in which the organisation shows up daily; and Soul to the sense of purpose that motivates the team. The company positions itself as a relationship-oriented consultancy that pursues measurable business outcomes rather than billable hours, and describes its competitive posture as agile and entrepreneurial relative to larger incumbents in the enterprise technology market.

4S4's stated origin was a recognition, accumulated over the founder's decade of experience in the enterprise technology sector, that clients were underserved by vendors focused on software delivery rather than authentic commercial partnership. The company was built to address this gap, initially with a focus on enterprise planning solutions and subsequently expanding into a broader portfolio of SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, and data analytics services.

The company serves organisations across South Africa and, by its own account, has expanded its client relationships to include some of South Africa's largest enterprises. It is actively developing capabilities in additional verticals including defence, retail, banking, fast-moving consumer goods, insurance, and manufacturing, with stated ambitions to expand beyond South Africa into international markets.

## Products and Services

4S4 organises its commercial offering into two principal divisions: Hybrid Application Consulting and System Integration. Within these divisions, the company delivers services across six primary practice areas.

**Application Consulting.** 4S4 provides functional and technical consulting services across major enterprise resource planning platforms, including SAP and Oracle. Engagements range from greenfield implementations and platform migrations to ongoing optimisation and enhancement of existing estates. The company offers dedicated consulting resources with expertise in financial management, procurement, supply chain, human capital management, and payroll across both SAP and Oracle product families.

**Application Managed Services.** 4S4 delivers a structured Application Management Support model designed to provide continuous functional and technical support for mission-critical enterprise applications. Under this model, clients receive a dedicated Customer Success Manager, defined service level agreements managed through ClickUp, 24/7 monitoring, proactive system management, patch management, database administration, service desk management, and user training. Transition services from incumbent service providers are included in the managed services scope. The company's managed services currently cover Oracle E-Business Suite, SAP FICO, SAP Analytics Cloud, and related modules.

**Mobile Application Development.** 4S4 offers end-to-end mobile application development services, supporting clients from conceptualisation through deployment on iOS and Android platforms. The company's development team is proficient in Swift, Kotlin, React Native, Flutter, and related technologies. Development engagements encompass requirements analysis, UX and interface design, application build, quality assurance testing, and app store deployment. The company additionally incorporates artificial intelligence, augmented and virtual reality, and Internet of Things capabilities into mobile application work where applicable.

**Cloud and Infrastructure Services.** 4S4 delivers a full spectrum of cloud and infrastructure solutions, including cloud strategy and migration services spanning public, private, and hybrid cloud environments; data centre audits and infrastructure engineering; network architecture and hardware provisioning; power and environmental systems planning; and managed infrastructure services including 24/7 monitoring, backup, disaster recovery, and lifecycle management.

**Talent as a Service (TaaS).** 4S4 operates a Resourcing as a Service model that provides clients with access to skilled IT consultants drawn from both local South African and offshore talent pools. Consultants are available with expertise in SAP, Oracle, Microsoft platforms, application development, integration services, and ETL and middleware technologies. The model is designed to be flexible and scalable, enabling clients to adjust resource levels in response to changing project demands without the overhead of permanent hires.

**Financial Operations and Consolidation Solutions.** Under its XP&A and Consolidation practice, 4S4 implements financial planning and consolidation platforms using SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Group Reporting, and SAP Business Planning and Consolidation. Services include implementation, system upgrades, health checks, and ongoing support. Delivered capabilities include unified planning and consolidation, legal and management consolidations, budgeting and forecasting, scenario modelling, and reporting across Microsoft Office, web, and mobile platforms with deployment options spanning both SAP HANA Enterprise Cloud and on-premises environments.

## Technology

4S4's technical capabilities are organised around four enterprise platform families — SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, and Alteryx — supplemented by partnerships with Snowflake, Neptune, and ATAJO.

**SAP.** The company holds deep expertise across the SAP product portfolio. In ERP, 4S4 supports SAP ECC environments and leads migrations to SAP S/4HANA, offering both cloud-based and on-premises implementation options. Its S/4HANA practice leverages the platform's in-memory processing capabilities to deliver real-time data analytics, streamlined financial and procurement processes, and enhanced supply chain visibility. In planning and analytics, 4S4 implements SAP Analytics Cloud for business intelligence, planning, and integrated financial reporting; SAP Business Planning and Consolidation for financial consolidation; and SAP Group Reporting for statutory close and disclosure. The company also provides comprehensive SAP Basis administration and SAP HCM implementations covering core HR, talent management, and payroll.

**Oracle.** 4S4 specialises in the Oracle SaaS portfolio, including Oracle Enterprise Performance Management Cloud, Oracle ERP Cloud, and Oracle Human Capital Management Cloud. In EPM, the company implements Oracle Planning and Budgeting Cloud Service, Oracle Financial Consolidation and Close Cloud, and long-range scenario modelling tools. In ERP, it covers Oracle's financial management, procurement, sourcing, and project financial management cloud modules. In HCM, 4S4 implements Oracle's core HR, talent management, recruiting, learning, payroll, and benefits modules. The company additionally maintains and optimises Oracle E-Business Suite estates, providing specialist support for EBS Financials, Supply Chain, and HR modules, and manages migration programmes from EBS to Oracle's SaaS platforms.

**Snowflake.** 4S4 is a technology partner of Snowflake, the cloud data platform. Through this partnership, 4S4 assists clients in unifying data from multiple sources, implementing scalable cloud-based data storage and compute environments, and enabling real-time analytics.

**Alteryx.** 4S4 has entered a formal partnership with Alteryx to deliver data analytics and automation solutions. The partnership enables 4S4 to offer clients access to Alteryx's code-free analytics and data preparation platform, with the aim of accelerating analytics workflows and supporting data-driven decision making.

**Neptune.** 4S4 partners with Neptune, a platform designed to bridge the divide between business users and information technology professionals. The partnership is focused on enabling organisations to build and deploy customised, scalable technology solutions with reduced time to market.

**ATAJO.** 4S4 has formed a partnership with ATAJO, a provider of mobile data collection and management solutions. The combined offering targets industries requiring in-field data collection for inspections, work orders, field sales, and asset management, with the objective of improving data quality and reducing decision-making risk associated with inaccurate field data.

The company's Technical Director has articulated a technology vision centred on deeper integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning into both client solutions and internal delivery processes, with an emphasis on seamless system integration and a strategic rather than transactional role for technology.

## Markets

4S4's primary market is South Africa, where it serves large enterprise clients across multiple sectors. The company has indicated intent to expand its market coverage to include defence, retail, banking, fast-moving consumer goods, insurance, and manufacturing sectors, in addition to its existing enterprise client base. Geographic expansion beyond South Africa is a stated strategic objective.

The company addresses organisations that operate complex enterprise software environments — primarily SAP and Oracle — and that require either implementation expertise, ongoing managed support, or specialist resource augmentation. It also serves organisations seeking to modernise legacy on-premises ERP systems by migrating to cloud-based SaaS platforms.

The company provides both local resources with in-depth knowledge of the South African regulatory and market environment, and offshore resources for clients requiring cost-effective or around-the-clock coverage.

## Operations

4S4's operational model is characterised by a lean organisational structure and a high degree of operational flexibility. The company frequently works through partner consulting houses in addition to deploying its own resources directly to clients, which it acknowledges creates a management requirement to balance agility with consistent service quality across varied client and partner contexts.

The Head of Operations oversees project delivery, service desk performance, resource allocation, and client onboarding. Delivery follows a structured project management approach with weekly client update cycles, formal blocker escalation, and defined phase gates. Support engagements operate on weekly client check-ins with joint prioritisation sessions. Capacity planning is conducted on a rolling weekly basis to account for leave, commitments, and unplanned demand.

The company has pursued internal operational modernisation through the automation of tasks previously managed in spreadsheets, the introduction of scrum delivery methodologies, and the integration of AI-assisted tools for workflow optimisation. ClickUp is the company's central platform for call logging, ticket tracking, and SLA management, providing both operational visibility internally and reporting transparency to clients.

The company self-describes its operational culture as agile, with a strong emphasis on ownership, transparency, and empowerment of team members. Cross-functional collaboration between the operations, sales, and delivery teams is a stated priority, with the Technical Director participating directly in pre-sales technical alignment and hands-on delivery problem-solving.

## Business Strategy

4S4's stated strategy is to evolve from a pure services business into a product and platform company. The company is developing proprietary tooling, with a particular focus on costing engines and analytics solutions tailored to complex supply chain and manufacturing operations. This internal product development is intended to differentiate 4S4 from conventional consulting firms and to create scalable intellectual property that can be delivered alongside its services.

The company's approach to client relationships is founded on long-term partnership rather than project-by-project engagement. It prioritises business value delivery over volume, and positions its smaller scale as an advantage — enabling greater responsiveness, ownership mentality, and willingness to engage directly on complex problems.

The company has pursued a deliberate partnership strategy, entering formal alliances with Snowflake, Alteryx, Neptune, and ATAJO to extend its technical coverage and create joint go-to-market opportunities. These partnerships allow 4S4 to offer clients a broader solution footprint without proportionally expanding its permanent headcount.

Talent development and organisational culture are treated as strategic priorities. The company invests in mentorship, knowledge-sharing, and an internal culture it characterises as familial — one intended to attract and retain personnel who treat the company's success as their own.

## Competitive Landscape

4S4 operates in the enterprise IT consulting and managed services market, where it competes against larger, established consulting firms with greater resources, brand recognition, and geographic reach. The company acknowledges this competitive context explicitly, characterising itself as a David among Goliaths and arguing that its agility, depth of ownership, and relationship-first approach make it more effective than larger competitors in its target segments.

The company's primary differentiators, as it describes them, are its willingness to pursue business outcomes over billable hours, its entrepreneurial speed of execution, its capacity for customised rather than templated solutions, and its end-to-end service coverage from strategic planning through technical delivery. The company also cites its active investment in proprietary tooling and AI-enhanced delivery processes as emerging competitive advantages.

Within its SAP and Oracle practice areas, 4S4 competes against both the global system integrators that dominate large enterprise SAP and Oracle implementations and against smaller boutique consultancies with narrower specialisations. Its Application Managed Services business competes with both large outsourcing providers and niche managed service providers.
